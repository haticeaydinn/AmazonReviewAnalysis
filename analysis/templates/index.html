  • 2. Bigram words graph: A 2-gram (which we’ll call bigram) is a two-word sequence of words like “higly recommend”, “love quality”, or ”bad smell”. You can see 20 most frequent 2-words pairs in a graph.
  • 3. Sentiment analysis: Customers might have positive, neutral or negative ideas about the product. We will analyze every reviews and give them an average polarity score. You can see the sentiment percentage and most positive/most negative 5 reviews. Note that when we calculate the polarity, we'll ignore neutral sentences.
